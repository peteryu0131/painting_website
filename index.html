Our Painting Process

From site review to final touch-up

A clear process helps each project move from free quote to visual preview, surface preparation, painting, cleanup, and final review.

01 Consultation & Review

We review photos or visit the site, confirm measurements, and understand the surface condition, goals, and timeline.

->
02 Quote & Visual Preview

You receive a clear quote. If you move forward, we can help preview color and finish direction before painting begins.

->
03 Preparation & Protection

Interior work includes floor and furniture protection, repair, caulking, sanding, and masking. Exterior work includes washing, scraping, priming, and full surface protection.

->
04 Painting & Final Check

We paint, clean the work area, review the finish with the owner, and complete workmanship-related touch-ups if needed.
