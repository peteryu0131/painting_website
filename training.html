Core studio modules

Curriculum

From wall prep to client-ready execution

The program is practical, visual, and built around real site habits instead of abstract classroom theory.

01

Foundation Wall Preparation

Inspection, patching, sanding, cleaning, priming, and masking before paint touches the wall.

02

Brush, Roller & Tool Control

Cut lines, roller pressure, coverage, edge discipline, tool care, and efficient room setup.

03

Decorative Finish Practice

Feature walls, layered movement, sample boards, finish consistency, and client preview basics.

04

Client Workflow & Site Standards

Jobsite protection, quoting basics, communication, timeline discipline, cleanup, and handover.

Who It Is For

Built for beginners and working painters

New painters who need a disciplined starting system.

Decorators who want stronger wall preparation and finish control.

Small teams who need consistent site standards and client workflow.

Future business owners who want to understand project delivery.
